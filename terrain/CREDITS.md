# Terrain

`zion.png` and `zion.json` are a 60 km square around Zion National Park, Utah,
made by `make-heightmap.py` from NASA's SRTM 1 arc-second (30 m) elevation
data, which is in the public domain, downloaded from OpenTopography
(https://opentopography.org). Until the real tile is in, the files are a
placeholder of fractal hills with a canyon, marked `"name": "placeholder"` in
`zion.json`.
