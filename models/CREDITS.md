# Models

`f16c.glb` is "F 16 C" (https://skfb.ly/6XHQo) by Usman Zia, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the original: converted from FBX to binary glTF, scaled to the
real jet's 15.06 m length, rotated nose-forward along −z with the wing plane at
y 0, the ambient-occlusion map baked into the colour texture at 1024 px, and
the normal and metallic maps dropped.
