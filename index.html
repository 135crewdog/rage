Credits

        

          
Flight model
The aerodynamic, engine and mass data are the F‑16 model in Stevens, Lewis and Johnson, Aircraft Control and Simulation (Wiley), which comes from NASA’s wind-tunnel and simulator tests published as NASA Technical Paper 1538 (Nguyen and others, 1979), a US Government work. The flight control system’s structure and limits are from public NASA reports; its gains are Rage’s own tuning.

          
Camera and HUD
After Ryan Vazquez’s flight-simulator series and its code, MIT licence.

          
Terrain
NASA’s Shuttle Radar Topography Mission elevation data, public domain, downloaded from OpenTopography.

          
The jet
“F 16 C” by Usman Zia, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, converted to glTF for Rage with its control surfaces cut out so they move.

          
3D
three.js, MIT licence.

135crewdog, with Claude Code.

        

      

    


    

      

        
Rotate your phone

        
Rage is flown in landscape. If it won't rotate, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center.

      

    


    

      

        
Motion access denied

        
Rage flies by tilting the phone, so it needs motion access. To fix it: close the app completely, open it again, tap Fly and choose Allow.
